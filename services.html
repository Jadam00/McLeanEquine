Riding Coaching

Overview

Structured, progressive coaching sessions covering flatwork and jumping for riders of all levels. As a BHS Stage 2 Coach, Sarah delivers lessons that are grounded in current best practice — building technical skill alongside rider confidence and horse understanding.

Lessons are tailored to your goals: whether that's improving your position and rhythm, developing your horse's way of going, building confidence after a setback, or working towards competition.

Who It's For

  • Complete beginners and nervous novices
  • Amateur riders looking to improve their flatwork or jumping
  • Competitors preparing for dressage, showjumping or eventing
  • Riders returning to the saddle after a break or difficult experience

What You Can Expect

  • Clear, structured progression built around your individual goals
  • Science-informed explanations — understanding the why, not just the how
  • Honest, constructive feedback delivered with patience and encouragement
  • Awareness of the horse's needs, not just the rider's

Example Scenario

A rider unsure how to improve their horse's canter transitions comes for a series of lessons. Sarah identifies a positional issue affecting the horse's balance and works systematically through body awareness exercises, canter preparation and transition timing. Within a few sessions, both horse and rider are more confident and consistent.

Coaching sessions are available across Oxfordshire and Berkshire. Enquire to discuss availability, location, and what you're looking to achieve.

Professional Horse Care

Overview

High-standard, welfare-focused horse care provided by a Level 3 Senior Equine Groom. From daily feeding, grooming and turnout to health monitoring, Sarah provides the kind of professional support that gives horse owners genuine peace of mind.

This service suits owners who need reliable, knowledgeable cover — whether that's a few days a week, for holiday periods, or as an ongoing arrangement.

Who It's For

  • Horse owners working full-time who need regular help
  • Owners who need trusted cover during holidays or illness
  • Those whose horses have specific care or medical requirements
  • Small private yards needing occasional professional support

What You Can Expect

  • Consistent, high-standard care delivered to your horse's individual routine
  • Welfare-focused approach — health changes noticed and communicated promptly
  • Experience with a wide range of horses, including competition horses and ex-racehorses
  • Flexible and reliable — a professional you can depend on

Example Scenario

A professional working long hours needs three morning visits per week to feed, muck out and turn out their horse, plus one evening check. Sarah provides consistent, reliable care and keeps the owner updated on their horse's condition — giving them confidence that their horse is in safe hands.

Care arrangements can be tailored to your schedule and your horse's needs. Get in touch to discuss what would work for you.

Schooling & Exercise Riding

Overview

Professional riding and schooling of client horses. Sarah is an experienced rider with competition background up to Novice dressage and 1m showjumping, and a track record working with horses from racehorses to riding school horses in all stages of training.

Whether your horse needs consistent flatwork to maintain their training, specific work on a problem area, or regular exercise to stay in condition, Sarah provides skilled, sympathetic riding tailored to the horse's stage and temperament.

Who It's For

  • Busy owners whose horses need regular, consistent ridden work
  • Horses with a specific training requirement beyond the owner's current level
  • Horses being brought back into work after a rest period
  • Competition horses that benefit from professional schooling between shows

What You Can Expect

  • Skilled, educated riding that respects the horse's way of going
  • Written or verbal feedback on progress after sessions
  • Science-informed training decisions — nothing forced or rushed
  • Experience with sensitive, sharp or challenging horses

Example Scenario

An owner's competition mare has become increasingly stiff through her back and resistant in transitions. The owner is away for work for extended periods. Sarah rides three times a week, introduces targeted suppling work and adjusts the training schedule — the mare's way of going improves noticeably over a six-week period.

Schooling arrangements are tailored to your horse's individual programme. Enquire to discuss frequency, goals and availability.

Competition Support

Overview

Professional on-the-day and pre-competition support for horse and rider. From plaiting and presentation through to warm-up riding or assistance at the show, Sarah draws on her competition experience to help you arrive at your class prepared, calm and confident.

Who It's For

  • Riders who want professional support managing nerves and warm-up
  • Horse owners competing without an experienced support team
  • Competitors preparing for their first affiliated show

What You Can Expect

  • Professional plaiting and turnout preparation
  • Warm-up riding or coaching on site, depending on your needs
  • Calm, experienced support — someone who's been in the arena

Ex-Racehorse Retraining

Overview

Specialist support for horses transitioning out of racing. Sarah's Diploma in Racehorse Care and Management, combined with direct experience exercising and preparing racehorses, gives her a genuine understanding of the racing environment and the specific challenges ex-racehorses face in their new lives.

Retraining is approached methodically, patiently and with a respect for the horse's intelligence and background. No shortcuts, no force — just systematic, science-informed progression.

Who It's For

  • Owners of off-the-track Thoroughbreds or other ex-racehorses
  • RoR (Retraining of Racehorses) affiliated owners
  • Those with ex-racehorses struggling to settle or adapt to a new routine
  • Owners who want an experienced set of hands for the early stages of retraining

What You Can Expect

  • Genuine understanding of the racing background and its physical/mental effects
  • Patient, methodical approach respecting the horse's pace of learning
  • Science-informed training — understanding how horses learn and respond
  • Honest progress updates and clear communication throughout

Example Scenario

A fresh off-the-track Thoroughbred arrives with high energy, no knowledge of basic flatwork aids and a strong forward default. Sarah works systematically through groundwork basics, introduces calm hacking, then begins developing ridden understanding of leg, rein and weight aids. Over several months the horse's topline develops, tension reduces, and the horse begins to show genuine aptitude for dressage work.

Every ex-racehorse is different. Get in touch to discuss your horse's background, current state and the kind of support that would be most useful.

Freelance Yard & Equestrian Support

Overview

Flexible, professional freelance support for private yards, livery yards, riding schools and private owners across Oxfordshire and Berkshire. Available for full-time, part-time or ad-hoc arrangements depending on your needs.

With experience managing large yards, teaching, and providing high-standard horse care, Sarah brings a level of breadth and professionalism that makes her a dependable addition to any equestrian operation.

Who It's For

  • Yards needing experienced cover for permanent or temporary staff absences
  • Riding schools looking for qualified freelance instructors
  • Private owners needing ongoing or occasional equestrian support
  • Livery yards wanting a known, reliable professional on call

What You Can Expect

  • Professional, senior-qualified support without the overhead of permanent employment
  • Broad experience spanning coaching, care, schooling and yard management
  • Reliable, consistent and communicative — someone you can depend on
  • Available in Oxfordshire and Berkshire; please enquire about specific locations

Example Scenario

A busy livery yard loses their head groom unexpectedly. Sarah steps in for a month to maintain standards, manage the daily routine, and provide care and schooling for livery clients' horses while a permanent appointment is found. The yard's standards are maintained throughout and clients remain confident in the service.

Freelance arrangements can be flexible and tailored to your operation. Get in touch to discuss your requirements.
