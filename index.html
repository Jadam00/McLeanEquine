Welcome

Where Passion Meets Evidence

McLean Equine is a Wantage-based equestrian business run by Sarah McLean — a BSc-qualified equine professional with a science-informed approach and a hands-on background spanning coaching, horse care, and competition support.

Whether you're a rider looking to develop your skills, a horse owner who needs dependable professional support, or a yard in need of experienced cover — McLean Equine offers flexible, qualified, and genuinely horse-first services across Oxfordshire and Berkshire.

Why McLean Equine

Qualified. Experienced. Horse-First.

Every service is underpinned by formal qualifications, real-world experience, and an unwavering commitment to the horses involved.

🎓

Academically Qualified

Holding a BSc (Hons) in Equine Science and currently working towards a PhD, Sarah brings a level of theoretical grounding that few freelance equestrians can match.

🏅

BHS Certified Coach

As a BHS Stage 2 Coach, Sarah teaches to the standards of the British Horse Society — the UK's leading equestrian authority. Coaching is structured, progressive and safe.

❤️

Horse-Welfare First

A Level 3 Senior Equine Groom qualification and a Diploma in Racehorse Care back up what is most important to McLean Equine: the health and wellbeing of every horse in its care.
